# CPC 2025 evidence ledger — revision record, Human Gate 1 (MODIFY)

**Revision date:** 13 October 2026. **Status:** **MODIFY — pending explicit human approval.** This document does not advance to additional investigation gates, Project 2 or Project 3.

## Reason for revision
The earlier ledger collapsed incompatible 2024 institutional reports into one exposure statement, treated a shareholder warning too loosely, called aggregate terminal liftings "counter-evidence", and combined two distinct unsupported claims: an observed national export decline and an attributable fiscal loss.

## Claim-by-claim changes

- **E03 → E03a + E03b (split; no automatic reconciliation).**
  - **KMG:** 55.7 million tonnes transported by CPC; 81% of Kazakhstan's reported total oil exports in 2024. Original: [KMG 2024 annual report](https://ar2024.kmg.kz/en/strategic-report/management-board-chairman-statement).
  - **KazTransOil:** 54.9 million tonnes via CPC; 80% of Kazakhstan oil exports; its neighbouring export-flow table totals 68.6 million tonnes for oil and gas condensate in 2024. Original: [KazTransOil 2024 annual report](https://kaztransoil.kz/en/to_shareholders_and_investors/annual_reports/?doc=2617&sc=ART).
  - The 0.8-million-tonne and one-percentage-point differences are **unresolved methodological/reporting differences**. The sources show substantial CPC concentration but do not justify presenting one reconciled national share.
- **E04 (tightened attribution and variable):** PJSC **Transneft**'s 18 February 2025 shareholder statement, hosted by CPC, reports that *CPC experts* expected 1.5–2 months of repairs and warned of a **possible** reduction of **oil throughput volumes from Kazakhstan by about 30%**. It does **not** document a 30% observed drop in Kazakhstan's national exports. The statement does not specify the denominator or complete comparison period. Original: [Transneft statement, hosted on CPC](https://www.cpc.ru/en/press/releases/2025/pages/20250218-1.aspx).
- **E06 (relabelled):** Aggregate terminal-lifting context, not proof for or against an event-specific loss. CPC says more than 28 million tonnes were lifted from 1 January through 23 May 2025, 1.4 million tonnes above the comparable prior-year period. Do **not** treat this as a post-event causal counterfactual. Original: [CPC shareholder meeting announcement](https://www.cpc.ru/EN/press/releases/2025/Pages/20250528.aspx).
- **E07 → E07a + E07b (split):** actual 30% national export decline and monetary/fiscal losses are different claims, each **NOT ESTABLISHED** by the primary records in this gate. The absence of evidence is **not** evidence of zero loss.

## Human Gate 1 unresolved questions

1. **E03a/E03b — comparability:** Are KMG and KazTransOil reporting the same geographical origin, volumes, crude/condensate scope, terminals, customs period and export denominator? What explains the 55.7 vs 54.9 Mt difference?
2. **E04 — basis of forecast:** What exact reference throughput and horizon did the roughly 30% prospective estimate refer to? Did CPC experts publish a quantitative engineering rationale? Did it cover an upper bound, base case, or a conditional scenario?
3. **E06 — physical vs fiscal consequence:** Can month/day data for Kazakhstan-origin oil and bypass operations isolate lost or deferred throughput, rather than year-on-year aggregate terminal liftings? What contemporaneous non-incident drivers (field production, nominations, maintenance, tanker availability, oil prices) differ?
4. **E07a — observed outcome:** Is there a verified time series for incident-specific Kazakhstan-origin CPC shipments and national oil exports, using consistent units and comparison dates? No percentage of observed national loss should be claimed without this.
5. **E07b — attribution:** If losses are established, what data on realised prices, royalties/taxes, contracts, operator costs, exchange rates and displaced vs delayed exports support a fiscal estimate? Which counterfactual and sensitivity bounds would make that estimate credible?

## Retained trust boundary
E01, E02 and E05 remain recorded as operator statements, not independently adjudicated full incident or economic-impact histories. Only claims E03, E04, E06 and E07 were revised in this Human Gate 1 update. The record is **awaiting a human decision**, and no subsequent agent phase was authorised.
